# 🌱 EcoChallenge App

The **EcoChallenge** is a gamified, educational web application designed to raise awareness about carbon footprint and promote sustainable living. Users calculate their daily carbon impact and complete eco-friendly missions to reduce it, all while enjoying interactive games, quizzes, and rewards.

## 🔧 Features

- 🌍 **Carbon Footprint Calculator**  
  Input daily habits and receive an estimate of your personal carbon emissions.

- 🧑‍🚀 **Personalized Avatar & Progress Tracker**  
  Track your eco-journey with a visual avatar and progress bar.

- 🌿 **Daily Eco-Missions**  
  Complete small, meaningful tasks each day to reduce your footprint.

- 🎮 **Mini-Games & Quizzes**  
  Learn through fun with short games and trivia about sustainability.

- 🗺️ **Story Mode**  
  Unlock chapters of an engaging eco-narrative based on your daily actions.

- 🔄 **Carbon Offset Suggestions**  
  Get tips on how to compensate for your emissions with real-world actions.

- 🏆 **Reward System**  
  Earn points, badges, and level up as you complete challenges and missions.

## 💡 Technologies Used

- **HTML5**, **CSS3**, **JavaScript**
- Responsive design for mobile & desktop
- Universal styling with `style.css`
- LocalStorage for progress tracking
